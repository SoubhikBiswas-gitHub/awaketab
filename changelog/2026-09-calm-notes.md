title: Notes that feel like a page, not a form
date: 2026-09-29
type: changed
---

Notes are now a calm writing page. The first line is the title, headings, lists and quotes have room to breathe, and ticking a checklist item draws the tick and settles the line.

The toolbar is gone. Select text to bold, italicise, strike, make a heading or a list. Type `/` for blocks: heading, checklist, bullet list, numbered list, quote or divider. Markdown typing and the keyboard shortcuts still work, and `Alt+F10` reaches the selection menu from the keyboard.

A small head row shows the note's name, a "Saved" dot and a ⋯ menu with Copy, Export as Markdown or plain text, Pin and Clear. Clearing or deleting asks first, then offers Undo for ten seconds.

An empty note offers three starters: To-do for this session, Meeting notes and Ideas.

Dictate sits at the bottom, where a thumb reaches it. While it listens, the button fills with your lamp colour and a small waveform moves with your voice, and the words appear softly at the cursor before they are typed in. The first time, a short card explains who turns speech into text. Browsers without speech recognition say so when you press it.

With Pro, your notes sit in a slim list beside the page on a desktop, and a swipe away on phones, with search, pins and dates such as "Today · 12:03 AM". Free users keep one note, with a quiet "More notes with Pro" in the ⋯ menu and a five-minute preview.

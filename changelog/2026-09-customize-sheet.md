---
title: One Customize sheet for face, look and sound
date: 2026-09-29
type: changed
---

The clock faces, the colour themes and the sounds now live together in one Customize sheet with three tabs: Face, Look and Sound. On a computer it opens at the side and the clock stays in view, so every pick shows on it at once; on a phone it rises from the bottom with a grab handle. Arrow keys move between the tabs, and the sheet opens on the tab you used last in this visit. Tapping the face name opens Face, and S opens Sound.

Look starts with the one-tap presets, then the theme (Auto, Light, Dark or OLED), colour theme, lamp colour and background. Sound has the end sound with its volume and a Play button, the focus sounds, the mixer and library, vibration and the tick-tock. Picking a face no longer closes the sheet, so you can compare faces in place. Pro items carry a small star and a "Try 5 min" button instead of a lock.

The popup now keeps one steady size and fits Chrome's popup limit in every state, so it no longer scrolls or jumps when you start or stop, including in a private window. While Chrome confirms a start, the popup already shows Stop, the length you picked and its time. In Settings, the language row lines up with the rest, the schedule steppers are full size, the From and To times stay together in narrow windows, and your licence reads "AwakeTab Pro · Chrome on macOS". The welcome page's buttons fit small screens, and its shortcut reads with "+" between the keys. The store screenshots are now saved without transparency, as the Chrome Web Store asks.

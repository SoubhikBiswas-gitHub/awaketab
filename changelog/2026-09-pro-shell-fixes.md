---
title: Pro pages and the site header hold together on small screens
date: 2026-09-27
type: fixed
---

On Manage devices, a loading state now holds the page's shape while your devices are checked, the offline message matches the one on the activation page, and keyboard focus stays on the page after you remove a device or activate a key. The licence key shown after checkout now wraps instead of being cut off on phones. The Pro page's schedule days stay easy to tap at 320 px. The header keeps the full AwakeTab name at every width. The small-screen theme button has its own tile. Translated pages now say which header links lead to English pages. The footer on the tool pages no longer sits twice as far below the content.

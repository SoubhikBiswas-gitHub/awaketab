---
title: Steadier layout, clearer focus and pages that stay in your language
date: 2026-09-26
---

The tool no longer shifts when a session starts. The status pill, the timer caption and the Stop button keep their places, so the page does not move under your finger. The "Read this in…" language suggestion now appears at the bottom of the screen instead of pushing the tool down.

Pages in other languages keep their address when the tool starts. Reloading `/es/` or a translated guide now keeps you on that page; before, it could switch you to English or to a page that does not exist.

Keyboard focus is now visible everywhere. That includes each part of the "Until…" time field and the drop-down menus in Settings in Safari. Pressing D several times in a row now steps through every theme; quick presses used to skip ahead by only one.

When the wake lock is blocked, the help link now says "Why this happens and how to fix it" instead of "Learn more".

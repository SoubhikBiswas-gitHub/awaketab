The first frame of the tool is now the finished one. The length the page starts with is already selected, the date and time line, the status pill and the Keep awake button are there from the start, and nothing fades or slides into place on load. When the browser declines to keep the screen awake before you have tapped anything, the tool stays calmly on Ready instead of showing a red "Blocked" pill. Switching themes no longer sets off a wave of transitions, and the resume card and notices only appear once their styles have loaded.

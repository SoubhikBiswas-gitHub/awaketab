title: A notepad with checklists and voice typing
date: 2026-09-28

The tool now has a notepad in a side sheet (a bottom drawer on phones), next to the clock, while the session keeps running. Open it with the Notes button under the actions on desktop, from Settings, or by pressing N. It has headings, bold, italic, lists and checklists, a small toolbar with keyboard shortcuts, and Markdown-style typing (# for a heading, - for a list, [ ] for a checklist). It saves as you type, counts words, copies to the clipboard and exports as .md or .txt.

Notes stay in this browser on this device. They are never uploaded and work offline.

In Chrome, Edge and Safari a mic button types what you say at the cursor, shows the words it is still hearing, and understands "new line", "new checklist item" and "stop listening". Before the first use it tells you that the browser sends your voice to its speech service (Google, Microsoft or Apple) to be transcribed; AwakeTab sends nothing. Esc or closing the notes stops listening.

One note is free. Pro keeps as many as you like, with titles, search and pins, and can be tried for five minutes. Notes you add in a preview are never deleted: afterwards you can still read, copy and export them, and editing them needs Pro.

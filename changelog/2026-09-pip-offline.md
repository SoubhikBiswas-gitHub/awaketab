---
title: The floating timer popup behaves the same offline in every language
date: 2026-09-27
type: fixed
---

When your browser has no built-in floating window, AwakeTab opens a small popup that mirrors your timer, and it is saved for offline use in your language when you first visit. If you are offline and the popup is asked for in a language that is not saved yet, AwakeTab now opens the saved popup in a language you have visited. Before, the English popup showed an error in that case and the other languages opened the full AwakeTab page inside the small window.

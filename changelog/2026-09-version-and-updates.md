---
title: The version on show, calm update notices and a mixer you can try
date: 2026-09-28
type: new
---

The footer and the bottom of Settings now show which version of AwakeTab you are using, such as "Version 2026.09.28 · 1a2b3c4", and Settings links to this page.

When a new version is ready, the tool says "A new version of AwakeTab is ready." with a Reload button. During a session it never reloads: it says "Reload when you're done" once, and offers Reload again when the session ends. Other pages show a small banner at the bottom with Reload and a close button.

The sound mixer can now be tried for five minutes without Pro, like the other Pro items. The mix you make in the preview is never saved, and when it ends the sound goes back to the one you picked.

The homepage has a new "Make it yours" section with the twelve faces, the colours and the sounds, and the Pro page, the docs and the credits on the about page list everything that came with this release.

title: Guides you can follow step by step
date: 2026-09-27
type: changed
---

Articles now open with a short answer under the heading. Set-up steps are numbered cards, device guides show the menu path for each step, and fix-it guides let you tick off each step as you go. Each page also has what its topic needs: a table of what the status pill means, a checklist before you start, a side-by-side comparison, or code you can copy. Nothing you tick is saved.

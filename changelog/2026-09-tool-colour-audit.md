date: 2026-09-29

A finished cook timer and success toasts now use the success green instead of the lamp colour, Night mode keeps every link and filled button in its red palette, and the Focus break colour, the ambient background and the theme-coloured browser bar follow your colour theme. Input borders in the dark theme are a little stronger, the Solarized light input fill is a touch lighter, and the preview chip sits on the card colour so "Keep it with Pro" stays readable with any lamp. Every text and control colour in the tool is now checked for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in all nine colour themes, twelve lamps and custom lamps, light, dark and OLED.

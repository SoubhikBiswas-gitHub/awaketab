---
title: The extension says "System awake" at System level
date: 2026-09-26
---

At System level, AwakeTab for Chrome keeps your computer awake, but your screen can still dim, turn off or lock. The popup used to say "Screen awake" there as well. It now says "System awake", with "Screen may dim or lock" underneath, in all eight languages. The toolbar badge still shows SYS. At Screen level nothing changes.

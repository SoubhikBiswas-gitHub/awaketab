---
title: Buttons and corners match everywhere
date: 2026-09-29
---

Every button now has the same rounded corners, from the small header buttons to Keep awake and Stop, in the web app and the Chrome extension. Chips, switches and the status pill stay round, so a round shape always means a choice or a status.

Chosen chips, tabs, menu rows and tiles now carry a solid lamp-coloured outline that is easy to see in every theme, including the theme switch and the settings bars.

Buttons come in three sizes. The buttons on the home page, Pro and the extension page are one size smaller than before, and the Chrome and Edge buttons are now the same height. Cards, panels and dialogs follow one corner scale, and page sections are spaced the same way on every page.

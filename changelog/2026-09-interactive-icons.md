---
title: Cards and icons that answer your pointer
date: 2026-09-28
type: changed
---

With a mouse, the cards on the homepage, the use case and device hubs, Guides, Compare and Docs now lean slightly toward the pointer, and a soft glow in your lamp colour follows it. Each use case and device icon acts out its job once when you point at or tab to its card or menu row: the pot steams, the page turns, the download arrow drops into the tray, the phone screen wakes. In the header menus, the ring around each use case fills to its suggested length. With the keyboard, a focused card lifts slightly instead of tilting, and on touch screens a card dips briefly when pressed. Nothing moves while you are not pointing at it, and nothing moves at all when your system asks for reduced motion.

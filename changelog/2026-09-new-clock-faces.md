---
title: Eight new clock faces, all free
date: 2026-09-28
type: new
---

The tool now has twelve clock faces. Next to Ring, Bold, Horizon and Tide you can pick Flip (split-flap cards that really turn), Rolling (odometer digits), Analog (a dial with a sweeping second hand and your time left on the bezel, in a Minimal or Luxe style), Rings (three activity-style rings), Words (a letter grid that spells the time), Nixie (glowing tubes), LCD (a seven-segment display) and LED (a dot-matrix panel). Open them from the face gallery (the face switch above the clock) or from Settings → Clock face. Each one shows your session's time and follows the lamp colour, the light, dark and OLED themes and the paused and blocked states like the others. They load only when you choose them, and with reduced motion they update without flipping, rolling or sweeping.

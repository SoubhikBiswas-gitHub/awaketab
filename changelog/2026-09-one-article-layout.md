---
title: Every guide reads the same way, with real browser logos
date: 2026-09-29
type: changed
---

Every guide, device page, comparison and doc now follows one layout: what it is, the answer, when the facts were checked and how long it takes to read, the steps, what it can't do, then questions and related pages. Use case and device pages put the tool right under the introduction; comparisons, fixes and docs put it after the steps. On a phone, "On this page" opens from the top. Wherever a browser or system is named, its logo now sits beside the name, and the device cards show the logos of the system and browser they cover.

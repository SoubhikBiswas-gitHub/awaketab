---
title: Focus sounds, more end sounds and a tick-tock
date: 2026-09-28
type: new
---

A new Sounds panel opens from the sound button in the tool header, with the S key, or from Settings. It plays seven focus sounds made live in your browser: brown, pink and white noise, rain, a café, a fireplace and a slow lo-fi loop. Each one fades in and out gently and, unless you turn it off, fades out when your session ends. In most browsers, media keys and headphone buttons can play and pause it. Pro adds a mixer, so you can layer several sounds at their own levels, and a lo-fi library that will hold recorded tracks as we add them.

Settings → End sound now offers Chime, Bell, Soft, Digital and Birds, all free, with a volume and a Play button. You can also have your phone vibrate at the end, and turn on a quiet tick-tock that runs while a session does and stops when the tab is hidden.

Sound never keeps the screen awake: the status pill is still the only sign that it is. Nothing plays or downloads until you ask for it, and if your browser needs a tap first, the panel says "Tap to start sound".

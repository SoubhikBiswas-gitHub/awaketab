Twelve clock faces, colour themes, focus sounds, a notepad and focus tools, all around the same honest wake lock. Keeping the screen awake stays free, and so does every new face and focus sound. Each feature loads only the first time you use it, so the tool is as quick to open as before.

Twelve clock faces: Flip, Rolling, Analog, Rings, Words, Nixie, LCD and LED join a redrawn Ring, Bold, Horizon and Tide. Tap the face name above the clock for a gallery of live miniatures, swipe sideways on the clock, or press `C` (`Shift+C` goes back). All twelve are free and follow your lamp colour.

Colours and backgrounds: Clear Night, Paper and Nord, the Aqua, Violet, Amber and Teal lamps, three backgrounds and three presets are free. Pro adds six themes, eight lamps, a colour of your own, five backgrounds such as Aurora and Stars, and five presets. Every theme has a light and a dark version.

Sounds for focus: brown, pink and white noise, rain, a café, a fireplace and a lo-fi loop, made live in your browser. Five end sounds, vibration and a quiet tick-tock are free too. Open Sounds with the sound button or `S`. Pro adds a mixer and a lo-fi library. Sound never keeps the screen awake; the pill still does the telling.

A notepad beside the clock: checklists, headings, export and voice typing, a keypress away with `N`. Notes stay in this browser and work offline. One note is free, and Pro keeps as many as you like.

Focus tools: Focus mode has Pause and Skip, and with Pro the Pomodoro repeats on its own. Write an intention with `I`, breathe along with `B`, and show a second time zone under the clock.

Try Pro for 5 minutes: any Pro look, the mixer, more notes, the repeating Pomodoro and a shared Message link can each be tried for five minutes, as often as you like. A small bar counts down, and nothing is saved.

Updates you can see: the footer and Settings show which version you have, with a link to what's new. When a new version is ready, AwakeTab says so and offers Reload, but never in the middle of a session.

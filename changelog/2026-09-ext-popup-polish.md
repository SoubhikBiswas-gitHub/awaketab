---
title: The Chrome extension popup says when it gets no answer, and keeps keyboard focus in view
date: 2026-09-28
type: fixed
---

If the extension's background doesn't answer, the popup now says so under the status pill and tells you how to fix it, instead of quietly showing Ready. Keyboard focus no longer gets lost when the Until panel or the Time's up card closes, and Escape on the Pro panel returns you to the row that opened it. The ring starts its slow sweep as soon as you press Start. Buttons, chips and settings rows now respond when you point at them, and the focus ring on the Settings icon and the schedule steppers is no longer cut off.

---
title: Settings fold into four groups that show what is set
date: 2026-09-29
type: changed
---

Settings is no longer one long scroll. It opens on four groups, Timer, Clock, Focus, and Device and privacy, and each one shows its current values in a line under its name ("Ask to extend · Chime · No notification"), so you can see how AwakeTab behaves without opening anything. One group opens at a time. Every control and every stored choice is the same as before. Reset to defaults now asks inside the sheet and says which things stay (your notes, stats and Pro). New: a Reduce motion switch stills the animations on this device even when your system setting allows them. Face, colours and sounds have moved to Customize; a row at the top of Settings takes you there.

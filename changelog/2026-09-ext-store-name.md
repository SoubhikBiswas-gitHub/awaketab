---
title: 'The extension is now called "AwakeTab: Keep Screen Awake"'
date: 2026-09-27
type: changed
---

The browser extension has a new name: "AwakeTab: Keep Screen Awake". You will see it in Chrome's extensions menu, on the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons listings, and on the welcome page that opens after you install it. The name is the same in every language. Nothing else changes: your settings stay as they are, and a Pro key you already activated in the extension keeps working and still shows up as the extension on your Manage page.

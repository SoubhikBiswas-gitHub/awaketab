---
title: Calmer sheets and dialogs, a stats dashboard and a grouped shortcuts list
date: 2026-09-29
type: changed
---

Every sheet and dialog now shares one header (title, optional line of help, one Close), one padding scale and one footer: actions sit at the end of the row on wider screens and stack full width at the bottom of the sheet on phones. Sheets open onto themselves instead of lighting up the Close button, and focus goes back to where you were when they close.

- **Share** shows what the link opens before you send it: the session length and whether it waits for a tap or starts at once, then the link with Copy beside it.
- **Your stats** leads with this week's time in large numbers, then today, your streak and the total; switch between a bar per day for the last week and the 12-week heatmap.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** are grouped into Session, Screen and Tools; the desktop overlay no longer squeezes its labels into one word per line.
- **AwakeTab Pro** is a short value card with the price and what Pro adds.
- **Until a time** and **Custom length** now say when the session ends ("ends at 5:30 PM", with "tomorrow" past midnight) right under the picker.
- **The rating prompt** starts as a small card with just the stars; the note and Send appear once you pick a star.

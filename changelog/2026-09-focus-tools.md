---
title: A Pomodoro that repeats, an intention, a breathing guide and a second time zone
date: 2026-09-28
type: new
---

Focus mode now has Pause and Skip. Pause stops only the timer: the screen stays awake and the status pill says so. Set the focus, break, number of cycles and long break in Settings → Focus tools. Each change of phase plays your chosen end sound. With Pro, blocks can repeat on their own after the long break until you stop, and every finished block counts in your stats. You can try this for 5 minutes without Pro.

Breathe is a new ambient mode (press `B`). The ring grows as you breathe in and settles as you breathe out, using 4-7-8 or box breathing. With reduced motion it shows the words and a simple bar instead.

Type what you're working on, from the Intention button beside the dock, from Settings, or with `I`. It shows quietly under the clock and in the status pill's tooltip. Clear it any time.

Add a second time zone under the clock, such as "London 3:15 PM · −4:30", by searching your browser's own list of zones. It says "tomorrow" when that zone is already on the next day.

New shortcuts: `T` Focus mode, `B` Breathe, `I` intention.

title: Colour themes, twelve lamps, backgrounds and presets
date: 2026-09-28
type: new
---

Settings → Appearance now has colour themes, more lamp colours, backgrounds and one-tap presets, each shown as a small live preview. Clear Night, Paper and Nord are free, and so are the Aqua, Violet, Amber and Teal lamps, the Grain, Dots and Grid backgrounds and the Classic, Library and Fjord presets. Pro adds Solarized, Midnight, Forest, Sunset, Mono and High contrast, eight more lamps, a custom lamp colour, the Contours, Waves, Aurora, Stars and Drift backgrounds, and presets such as Night desk and Northern lights. Every theme has a light and a dark version, stays readable (WCAG AA) with every lamp, and appears on the first frame with no flash.

Any Pro look, and a shared Message-mode link, can be tried for five minutes as often as you like. A small bar counts down, a notice comes at one minute, and then the page fades back to your last free choice. A preview is never saved and never touches the wake lock or the timer, and while a session runs it never shows a Pro link.

Fixed: choosing Message mode in Settings without Pro no longer saves it as your mode.

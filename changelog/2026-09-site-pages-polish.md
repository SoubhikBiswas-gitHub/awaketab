---
title: Site and Pro pages line up with the design
date: 2026-09-27
type: fixed
---

The About, Changelog, Privacy, Terms, Kiosk, Library, Embed and Pro pages now follow the Clear Night design more closely. Prices use even-width digits, the device table on Manage devices keeps each date on one line, the history preview on the Pro page says in plain view that its numbers are sample stats, and changelog filters no longer cut off their labels.

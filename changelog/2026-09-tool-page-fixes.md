---
title: The tool fits small phones and landscape screens, and says when Pro lapses
date: 2026-09-28
type: fixed
---

On short phones (375 × 667, 360 × 740) and small tablets the clock face now shrinks so Stop and the time's-up buttons stay on screen, and landscape phones under 800 px wide no longer scroll sideways or squeeze the header. The other lengths below a preset page switch a running session in place instead of reloading the page, and the last-minute glow no longer scrolls the page sideways. The tool now shows a notice when a yearly Pro renewal fails or Pro has ended on this device, and the language suggestion is written in the language it suggests.

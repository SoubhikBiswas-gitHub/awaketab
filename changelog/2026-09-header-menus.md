---
title: Header menus for what you use AwakeTab for, your device and further reading
date: 2026-09-28
type: new
---

The site header now has three menus: Use it for (six common jobs, each with its suggested length drawn as a small ring, and a shortcut to start a 30-minute session), Devices (iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows 11 and Chromebook) and Resources (guides, docs, comparisons and the changelog). They open and close with the keyboard, Esc or a click outside, with no script. On phones and on the tool page the same menus sit behind one Menu button, as a sheet at the bottom of the screen on phones. The header's add button says Add to Chrome, Add to Edge on Edge, and Add to Home Screen on iPhone and iPad. Header, page content and footer now line up on one edge at every width.

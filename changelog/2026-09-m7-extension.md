---
title: AwakeTab for Chrome, the browser extension
date: 2026-09-26
type: new
---

A web page can only keep the screen awake while its tab is visible. AwakeTab for Chrome asks Chrome itself to keep the display — or just the computer — awake, so it keeps working when the tab is hidden or the window is minimised, for as long as Chrome is running. It still can't stop sleep when you close a laptop lid, and it never fakes keyboard or mouse input.

Pick Screen or System, then a preset from 15 minutes to 4 hours, a time, or until you stop. The popup shows the same ring and status as the web app and only says "Screen awake" once Chrome has accepted the request. The toolbar badge shows ON, SYS or the minutes left, and Alt+Shift+A toggles it from any tab. When a timer ends you can get a notification with +30 min and Stop buttons.

With Pro, the extension adds weekly schedules and auto-start when Chrome opens or when you visit a site you choose; your Pro key activates it as one of your five devices. It needs only the power, storage and alarms permissions; notifications and site access are optional and asked for one at a time. Anonymous usage statistics are off by default. There's a new page at /extension, and the privacy policy has an extension section.

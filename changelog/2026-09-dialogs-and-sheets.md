---
title: Settings, stats, share and the language picker open and close more calmly
date: 2026-09-28
type: fixed
---

Every sheet and dialog now works the same way. On phones they rise from the bottom within reach of your thumb, stay above the on-screen keyboard and the home bar, and a tap outside closes them. The page behind no longer scrolls while one is open, closing one returns you to the button that opened it, and opening Stats or Pro from Settings swaps one sheet for the other instead of stacking them. Sheets slide out as gently as they slide in, and never show before their styles have loaded on a slow connection. The language picker at the bottom of every page is a proper dialog on phones, so screen readers and the keyboard stay inside it.

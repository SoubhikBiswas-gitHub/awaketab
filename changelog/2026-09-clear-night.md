title: A new look, Clear Night
date: 2026-09-27
type: changed
---

AwakeTab has a new look. The header, footer, guides, site pages, Pro pages, embed widget, extension and awake-screen modes are redesigned, with Light, Dark and OLED themes that follow your system unless you pick one. The site now uses the Geist and Space Grotesk typefaces, served by AwakeTab itself, so no font request leaves the site.

The awake-screen modes (Clock, Focus, Minimal, Night, Message and Cook) and the floating window have a calmer layout; in Cook mode the whole timer card is the pause button. The embed widget's compact size is now 320 × 104, and its credit link sits below the widget instead of inside it.

/pro now shows a lamp picker that recolours the page, a three-question plan helper that often answers "free", and live previews of Message mode, 12 weeks of stats and extension schedules. Features that are not built yet are gone from the copy, and Embed licences say "open soon". After checkout, /pro/activate says "Pro is active" with next steps instead of jumping to the tool, and has its own pages for a closed checkout, a failed payment and a missing key. Manage devices shows how many of your five devices are in use, marks this device, asks before removing one, says so when it cannot reach the server, and explains a renewal that did not go through or a plan that ended. Devices get readable names such as "Chrome · macOS", and the Pro pages respect the telemetry switch.

---
title: Notices you can read, even over a sheet
date: 2026-09-28
type: fixed
---

The small notices on the tool no longer flicker or restart every second while a session runs, and they stay on screen as long as the text needs. They wait while you point at them, focus them or look at another tab, so none vanish unseen. A notice now shows above an open sheet or menu instead of hiding behind it, each kind has its own icon shape, and the "Something went wrong" notice offers Retry. Screen readers hear each notice once.

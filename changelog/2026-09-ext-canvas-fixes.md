---
title: Clearer times and tidier pages in the Chrome extension
date: 2026-09-27
type: fixed
---

The extension now writes every time the same way, such as 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and the week chart in Settings marks Noon. When auto-start keeps your device awake with no end time, the popup shows how long it has been running and since when, and a scheduled session names its days. Adding a new schedule with the same hours as a saved one no longer hides the saved one's label. The Privacy policy and What's new links are underlined again, the Settings page has the spacing it was designed with, and Settings and the welcome page fit better in medium-width windows. In the store screenshots, the toolbar badge now matches the time left in the popup.

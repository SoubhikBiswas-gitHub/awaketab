title: Guides rewritten and corrected
date: 2026-09-26
---

Twenty-five guides were rewritten from scratch, including a new page for teachers at /for/classroom. The other guides stay online but out of search results until they are rewritten.

Corrected: earlier versions of many guides said that battery saver, Energy saver or Low Power Mode makes the browser refuse to keep the screen on. Chrome's and Safari's source code has no such check. What really stops it: a hidden tab, a site setting that blocks it, Safari before your first tap, or Firefox at 5 % battery or less. We also removed lines that described device tests we have not recorded yet.

Some pages moved: the Windows 10 page is now part of "Windows 11 and 10", the second-monitor page is now the guide "Second monitor keeps turning off: how to fix it", the Modern Standby page is part of "Lock screen versus display sleep", and "NoSleep.js vs wake lock" is part of the NoSleep.js comparison. The navigation, live-stream, online-exam and baby-monitor pages were removed.

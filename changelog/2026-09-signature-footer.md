title: A new footer
date: 2026-09-29
type: changed
---

Every page ends with a new footer. A slightly tilted band lists what AwakeTab does, and it slides along as you scroll to it. A large outlined "AWAKETAB" sits faintly behind the links, and a line of light passes over it as you arrive. On the tool page its dot lights up in the lamp colour while the screen is awake, amber when paused and red when blocked, like the logo. Nothing moves once you stop scrolling, and nothing moves at all when your device asks for reduced motion.

Each link now has a small icon, and the device links show the Apple, Android, Windows and Chrome logos. On phones the five groups open one at a time in a single rounded list.

The bottom row shows "© 2026 AwakeTab · MIT", the version, a "Made with care · Open source on GitHub" link, the language switcher and a Back to top button. Back to top returns to the top of the page and puts keyboard focus back at the start of the content.

title: Calmer controls on the tool
date: 2026-09-29
type: changed
---

The tool page asks one question at a time. Before a session there is one row of lengths (15 min to 4 h and "Until I stop") and a ⋯ at its end for "Until a time…" and "Custom…", then the one "Keep awake" button. Once the screen is awake, the row folds into one line, such as "Ends 1:31 AM · Change", and "+15 min", "+30 min" and a quieter Stop take its place. Nothing moves when a session starts or stops.

The header is quieter too. It shows the time with seconds and the date, a Customize button for faces, colours and sounds, and a labelled More menu with Share, Floating window, Your stats, Keyboard shortcuts, Settings and the guides, each with its key. The sound icon appears only while a sound plays. The theme moved to Customize, and D still switches it. The header stays at the top when you scroll and turns solid so the page reads under it.

The face name and arrows now sit right under the clock, with a small lamp dot that opens the colours. "How do we know?" is a small info button beside the status. Notes and Intention are quiet buttons whose keys show when you point at them.

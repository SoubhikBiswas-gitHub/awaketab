---
title: The footer is now a site map
date: 2026-09-28
type: changed
---

Every page now ends with a footer that lists the whole site in five columns: Product, Use it for, Devices, Resources and About, next to the AwakeTab promise and the open source licence. On phones the columns fold into tap-to-open sections. Keyboard shortcuts in the footer opens the tool's shortcut list, the GitHub repository is linked, and the language switcher and the "No ads on the awake screen, now or later." promise stay where they were.

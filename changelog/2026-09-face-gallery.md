---
title: A clock face gallery, swipe to switch, and redesigned Ring, Bold, Horizon and Tide
date: 2026-09-28
type: new
---

Choosing a clock face is now a gallery. The face switch above the clock names the face in use; its arrows step to the previous or next face, and tapping the name opens a sheet with all twelve faces as live miniatures, grouped as Classic, Retro and Modern and shown in your current state, lamp colour and theme. A miniature plays when you point at it or move to it with the arrow keys, and the Analog tile has its Minimal and Luxe styles right there. On a phone or a trackpad you can also swipe sideways on the clock itself, and C on a keyboard moves to the next face (Shift+C goes back). A switch never leaves the box empty: the old face stays until the new one is ready.

The four original faces were redrawn. Ring has finer minute ticks with hour marks, a soft glow inside the dial and a sweep that circles once a minute, holding still while paused. Bold has much larger numerals with the seconds set small beside them, a slimmer progress bar that shows paused and blocked like the ring, and a clearer "until" line. Horizon has richer skies for dawn, day, dusk and night, a dotted path showing where the sun or moon will set when your session ends, layered hills and reflections on the water. Tide has deeper water, a bright surface line and slower, gentler waves.

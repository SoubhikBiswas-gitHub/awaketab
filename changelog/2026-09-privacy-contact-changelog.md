The [About](/about) page now lists a contact address, support@awaketab.com, for support, refunds, bug reports and data requests.

The [privacy policy](/privacy) now says what AwakeTab keeps on its servers and for how long. Anonymous usage events are deleted after 90 days. Ratings carry no identity and are kept for up to 2 years. A licence record is kept until the licence expires plus one year. It also explains how to have your licence data deleted, and describes the article ads before they start (none are shown yet).

This changelog now shows formatting and links properly and lists entries newest first.

About this page

Keep your screen awake for 30 minutes

Tap “Keep awake · 30 min” above and this screen stays on for the next 30 minutes, as long as this tab stays visible. Before you start, the tool shows the time it will stop, so you know when the screen may dim again.

When the 30 minutes are up, AwakeTab asks “Time's up. Keep going?” with +15 min, +30 min, +1 h and Stop. If you do nothing, it stops on its own and your usual screen timeout takes over. To change the length, pick another preset in the tool or one of the lengths below; a change during a session applies straight away.

Trust the pill, not the button. It reads “Screen awake” only after the browser confirms the wake lock, and it changes to “Paused — tab hidden” the moment you switch away.

Honest limit

Keep this tab visible and the device open. A hidden tab, a closed laptop lid or a work sign-in policy can still let the display sleep or lock. AwakeTab never simulates input, so it will not keep Teams or Slack showing you as active.

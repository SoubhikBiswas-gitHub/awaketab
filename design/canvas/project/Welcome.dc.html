AwakeTab is ready. Pin it where you can see it.

It keeps your screen, or your whole computer, awake for as long as you choose, and it says plainly whether that is working. Pinned, its icon shows the time left at a glance.

What happens when you use it

Three moments, start to finish.

  1. 1
    15 min 30 min 1 h ∞
    Until 6:00 PM

    Pick how long

    Click the icon and choose a length, or an end time like 6:00 PM. Start uses your default length.

  2. 2
    25m
    Screen awake Tab hidden · window minimised

    It holds while you work

    Switch tabs, hide the window, minimise Chrome. The badge counts down, and it only appears while the device is really being kept awake.

  3. 3
    AwakeTab Your 1 h session is done +30 minStop

    It tells you when time is up

    Turn on notifications in Settings and Chrome shows one when the session ends, with +30 min and Stop. The popup also offers more time for five minutes.

Screen or System

The popup has one switch for how awake to keep things. Screen is the default. You can change it any time, even mid-session.

Keep your screen on while cooking

Phones dim and lock after 30 seconds to a few minutes without a touch, and nobody wants to wake a screen with floury fingers. AwakeTab asks your browser for a screen wake lock, so the display stays on for as long as you cook. It works while this tab is on screen, next to the recipe or on its own, and the pill in the tool tells you whether the lock is really held.

What to expect while you cook

Cook mode counts up from the moment the screen is held, so you can see how long the onions have had. Tap the big number to pause the count; the screen stays on while it is paused. Add up to three kitchen timers with the 5, 10, 15, 30 and 60 min buttons. Each one counts down on its own, chimes at zero and flashes for 10 seconds, and it keeps running while the count is paused. Cook mode has no end time, so no end-of-session prompt interrupts a recipe.
